# Clause graphs of tight formulas — checks behind Lemma 7 and Remark 8 (v1.1.0)

The *clause graph* D of a tight formula F has a vertex for each of the 20 clauses and an edge for each of the 30 literals, joining the two clauses that contain it; F is D together with the pairing of its edges into pairs {x, ¬x}. D is a cubic graph on 20 vertices, simple by Lemma 7(a) of the note (no two clauses of an unsatisfiable formula of this kind share two literals), which also gives girth ≥ 5 for G(F). Two clauses are *twins* when their three literals continue into the same three other clauses (an induced K_{2,3} in D). The scripts here were written independently of `verification/census/` (a separate session, 22 September 2026; see the file headers) and are run against `census_tight_15_20.json`.

Python 3, numpy, networkx, pynauty; nauty's `geng` for the two graph lists.
